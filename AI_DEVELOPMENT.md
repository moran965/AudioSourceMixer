# AI-assisted development

Audio Source Mixer was built through vibe coding and iterative AI-assisted development under human direction.

The human maintainer defines product requirements, chooses trade-offs, reviews repository changes, operates the application, and owns release decisions. AI tools have assisted with implementation, tests, documentation, investigation, and repetitive repository work. AI is not presented as a legal author, copyright holder, maintainer, or quality guarantee.

Automated tests validate deterministic software behavior, but they cannot prove that a tone reached a particular physical speaker, that a Bluetooth reconnection is acceptable, or that every driver behaves correctly. Those claims require recorded, hands-on testing with the identified browser and hardware. Release notes must distinguish automated, API-level, endpoint-meter, and human listening evidence.

Contributors may use AI tools, but remain responsible for every submitted line. Review generated code for correctness, security, privacy, licensing, provenance, unnecessary dependencies, and accidental disclosure. Do not submit prompts, tool caches, browser profiles, credentials, private logs, or generated assets whose rights are unclear.

Security and release authority always remains with a human. AI tooling must not publish a binary, approve its own change, claim a signature or hardware result it did not observe, or bypass account and operating-system protections.
