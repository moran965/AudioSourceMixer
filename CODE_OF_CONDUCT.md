# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Protect other people's privacy and safety.
- Focus discussion on what is best for the project and its users.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), marking the report as a conduct matter. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and protect reporter privacy.
